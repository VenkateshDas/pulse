How we handle your information

A few things you might be wondering.

Can I download Pulse today?

This website is focused on the upcoming early-access experience. We’re gathering interest before inviting people to a public build. There is no download offered here yet.

Is the preview showing my actual Mac?

No. The preview uses illustrative data to explain the experience. It doesn’t inspect your files, measure your system or perform cleanup.

Which Macs are you building for?

We’re developing for macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel. We’ll confirm supported hardware and any feature limitations with the early-access invitation.

Will Pulse be free?

Joining the waitlist will be free. Launch pricing hasn’t been decided. We’ll explain any pricing before you choose to buy.

Does cleanup always make a Mac faster?

No. Storage usage and performance are different questions. Removing files may help with storage, but it doesn’t necessarily reduce CPU usage or make an app faster.

What happens after I join?

Once signup opens, you’ll confirm your email address and receive an invitation when early access is ready. We may ask about the Mac problems you’d like help with. You’ll be able to unsubscribe from our emails.

A clear start, including your privacy.

This preview doesn’t access your Mac’s files or system readings. This version of the page has no analytics or session-recording scripts. Signup isn’t collecting email addresses until the early-access list opens.

When signup opens, the linked form will explain how your information is handled. We’ll update this notice before enabling signup or analytics.
