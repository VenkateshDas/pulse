Website privacy

Updated 1 November 2026. This website does not inspect your Mac’s files or system readings.

Website measurement

Website analytics are optional and stay off unless you allow them. The homepage offers the measurement controls. These Guides and privacy pages do not send measurement events.

If you allow analytics on the homepage, PostHog processes page visits, broad referral sources and selected button clicks using a random browser identifier. We do not send email addresses, typed text, URL queries or session recordings. IP storage is disabled. Your choice and, if allowed, the random identifier are saved in this browser. See PostHog’s privacy policy.

We respect Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. You can turn analytics off below. This stops future collection; it does not delete events already received.

Early-access signup

Our signup form is hosted by MailerLite. When you subscribe, MailerLite processes your email address and technical signup records, including IP address and confirmation activity, to manage the list and record your signup consent. We use this information for early-access invitations and occasional Pulse updates. This is separate from website measurement.

Unsubscribe using the link in our emails, or contact getpulseapp01@gmail.com to request removal. See MailerLite’s privacy policy.
