# Pulse

## Register

product

## Users

People using Macs for teaching, study, creative work, administration, business, and software development. They want to resolve storage and performance problems without learning system terminology.

## Product Purpose

Help people understand their Mac, choose an appropriate action, and see what actually changed. A healthy Mac should not need unnecessary intervention.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, capable. Familiar Mac interactions and honest explanations create confidence.

## Anti-references

Neon monitoring consoles, walls of gauges, small tracked uppercase text, unexplained acronyms, magic optimization claims, and misleading universal undo promises.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the person's task and an explanation.
- Reveal technical details when useful.
- Preview consequential actions and report actual outcomes.
- Make recovery limits explicit.
- Reuse native controls and preserve the lightweight monitoring architecture.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, meaningful VoiceOver navigation, readable contrast, larger text, reduced motion and transparency, and locale-aware content. Initial copy is English; localization readiness is not a claim of translated coverage.
