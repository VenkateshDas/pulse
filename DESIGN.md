# Pulse design system

Native macOS product UI. Follow system appearance. Preserve Pulse green with restrained, opaque surfaces and familiar controls.

## Color

| Role | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas | #F5F7F8 | #171D21 |
| Surface | #FFFFFF | #222B30 |
| Primary ink | #172126 | #F2F6F7 |
| Supporting ink | #4F6068 | #B5C2C9 |
| Pulse accent | #176B56 | #70D6B3 |
| Divider | #CCD5DA | #52616A |

Semantic colors have separate light/dark pairs. Status includes text and a symbol. Measure actual token combinations; body text targets at least 4.5:1. Primary buttons use contrasting ink appropriate to their fill. Alternate themes follow the same readability requirements.

## Typography and layout

System SF for content and controls; rounded SF only for the wordmark. Changing readings use monospaced digits. Titles 24 pt semibold, sections 17 pt semibold, body 13–14 pt, supporting labels at least 12 pt. Standard and Larger text settings share one layout.

Spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32 pt. Native split navigation and controls. Readable rows and dividers precede cards. Content group radius at most 12 pt; no decorative glowing shadows or nested cards. Compact windows stack detail layouts. Critical text wraps.

## Signature and interaction

A persistent inline outcome summary connects an action to its actual result and recovery route. Moving files to Trash does not mean storage has been freed. An observation does not prove performance improved.

No animations driven by sampled values. User-triggered transitions respect Reduce Motion. Content surfaces remain opaque; native materials have a Reduce Transparency fallback. Keep stable routing identifiers separate from localized presentation titles.
